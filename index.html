Why Yemoda stands out

Most project tools track what people say — status updates, tickets, meetings. Yemoda reads the signal at the source: every GitHub push is linked to the task it implements, diffs are AI-reviewed in the context of the project, and proposed fixes arrive ready to commit. Risk surfaces in reviewed diffs and slipping tasks — before it derails the roadmap.
